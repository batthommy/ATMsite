Where Do We Want to Go?

Hi, I’m Thomas, and if you are reading these lines, you have probably decided to discover more about ATM.

The truth is that ATM is still very small today. For now, there is just me, some of my friends, some ideas, many projects, and a strong desire to build something great. I do not know exactly where we will be in one year, five years, or ten years. No one can really know.

What I do know is that I do not want to stand still.

Every line of code I write is a small step forward. Every project teaches us something. Some work immediately, others take time, and others fail. But even when something does not go as planned, it always leaves a lesson that makes us better.

ATM was not created just to occupy a space on the Internet. It was created to create, experiment, and build. We like imagining things that do not yet exist and trying to turn them into something real.

Sometimes we learn from our mistakes. Other times we observe the mistakes of others and try to do better. In both cases, we continue to grow.

Maybe one day ATM will consist of more people. Maybe it will become a group of young people with the same passion for technology, creativity, and the desire to create increasingly ambitious projects. Or perhaps it will take a different path. It is part of the journey.

For now, we are at the beginning.

And, to be honest, that is exactly the best part.

Because the future is not something you wait for.
It is built.
